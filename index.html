Summary

The game is an offline educational game that helps children learn English. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no user-generated content. We do not collect, store or sell personal information, and the developer has no server that receives data from the app.

Data stored on the device

Game progress (for example the player's chosen name, language, score and unlocked levels) is saved only in the app's private storage on the device. It is never sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob (a banner on the main menu and an optional rewarded video that the child chooses to watch for a free hint). Because the app is designed for children, we mark all ad requests as child-directed and for users under the age of consent, and we limit ad content to the "G" rating. Personalised advertising is not used.

To show ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process certain technical data on the device, such as the IP address, device and app identifiers, and general diagnostic information. This processing is carried out by Google under its own policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy. The app may also show a consent message (Google User Messaging Platform) where required by law.

Permissions

The app requests only internet and network-state access, used solely to load ads. The game itself works fully without an internet connection.

Children

The app is intended for children and families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the app contains no social features, chat, purchases or external links.
